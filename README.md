# Tidepool support

Tidepool renders Mermaid diagrams in Confluence and Jira, with AI help to draft and fix them.

- **Found a bug?** [Report it here](../../issues/new?template=bug.yml). Inside Tidepool, "Report a problem" fills in the technical details for you.
- **Have a question or idea?** [Ask a question](../../issues/new?template=question.yml) or [suggest a feature](../../issues/new?template=feature.yml).

Website: [tidepoolapp.dev](https://tidepoolapp.dev) · Email: support@tidepoolapp.dev · Security: security@tidepoolapp.dev (please don't open a public issue)

We usually reply within 2 business days. Issues here are public, so leave out anything confidential.
